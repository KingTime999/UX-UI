About Mixi Chicken

Mixi Chicken is a famous dried chicken brand inspired by streamer Độ Mixi (real name: Phùng Thanh Độ, born October 14, 1989), a Vietnamese streamer, YouTuber, and musician. The product is made from fresh chicken meat, combined with lemongrass and special spices to create an irresistibly delicious flavor.
